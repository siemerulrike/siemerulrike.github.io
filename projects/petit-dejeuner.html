Petit déjeuner
Sculpture / Installation 2025

Recreating a breakfast table entirely in papier-mâché: the table, coffee, cereal, milk, slices of bread and jam. This work explores everyday rituals, the ones some of us have and others don't. I wanted to romanticise this familiar moment, playing with the idea of romanticising everyday life, a trend often seen on social media. I imagined an idealised version of breakfast, perhaps the kind you might find in a children's book. A little blurry, a little dreamy, somewhere between memory and imagination. An aesthetic that exists in my mind, but that can never quite be found in real life.
