L'attente
Sculpture/Performance 2025

A performance built around sculptures of everyday objects, revisiting a moment we have all experienced countless times: waiting for a reply, a confirmation, a sign that something might happen. In French, l’attente means both waiting and expectation: what we hope for from someone, from a relationship, from life itself. Looking at our phones, we are constantly waiting for something. Waiting has become such a central part of our everyday lives, an almost familiar ritual. But what happens to all the time spent waiting?
