Dress-up
Sculpture/Performance 2025

etting dressed, wearing a dress, shoes and a bag made out of papier-mâché. Imagining the basic forms of things: how would you draw a dress, a shoe, a bag, so that everyone immediately recognises them? I let my intuition guide me towards the colours. Shoes in my imagination? They are red! The dress? Blue, and a little pastel pink for the handbag. When I was a child, I wasn’t allowed to have any Barbies. Sometimes I feel like my work makes me do things I wasn’t able to do as a child, like playing silly dress-up games.
