Sport
Sculpture/Performance 2025

What is sport all about? For some, it’s part of life; for others, it’s something that has to be done. In capitalist society, it costs a lot of money, and you can never do enough. I wanted to observe how people engage with sports, both online and in my everyday surroundings. I looked at people doing sports, trying to do sports, and showing how they do sports. I decided to replicate different objects and create a video performance. The lip-sync video “I Just Can’t Get Enough” can be found on my Instagram account.
