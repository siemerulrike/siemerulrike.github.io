Piknikdouille
Set-design/Performance 2025

A drag performance takes the form of a picnic in the park. In collaboration with drag artist Kidzmoon, the project brings together performance, storytelling and outdoor spectacle to create a playful experience. The setting is composed of colourful papier-mâché sculptures in different sizes, including a two-metre-high champagne bottle, oversized glasses and a collection of smaller picnic objects. Together, these elements transform an ordinary picnic into an unusual and colourful scene, where gestures and unexpected situations bring the story to life, somewhere between the everyday and the absurd.
